# Purchasing Clothes on Myntra

This topic describes how to purchase clothes on the **Myntra** app.

## Requirements
- Install the Myntra mobile app on your device
- Select your preferred language
- Sign in to your Myntra account (create one if you don’t have it)
- Verify your account using your registered mobile number or email
- Ensure a stable internet connection
- Ensure a valid payment method
- Ensure a delivery address is associated with your Myntra account

Note: If you are a new user, complete the account registration process.

## Steps
1. Open the **Myntra** mobile app.
   Result: The Home screen appears.

2. Type the clothing item name, brand, or category in the Search box and click **Search**.
   Result: The **Search Results** screen appears.

3. Select the **Product** you want to purchase.  
   Note:  
   - Select the size (XS, S, M, L, XL, XXL)  
   - Click Add to Bag to buy.
   - Click Wishlist to save for later

4. Review **Product Details** and click **Place Order**.  
   Result: The Select a delivery address screen appears.

5. Click **Continue** to select **Payment Options**.  
   Result: The **Payment** screen appears.

6. Review **Payment Details** and **Order Summary**.  
   Note: You can change the payment method if required.

7. Review item price, delivery charges, discounts, and order total.

8. Click **Continue** to pay the amount.

9. Verify order completion.  
   Result: The order is placed successfully. The confirmation page displays the estimated delivery date and a confirmation message.
